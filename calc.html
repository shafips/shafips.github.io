How it works

You are paid a fixed base for every hour worked, plus a performance amount that depends on how much you produced in those hours. Each hour is expected to produce a set amount of output. Produce it, and you get the full performance amount. Produce less, and the performance amount is reduced by the same proportion.

The three steps

  1. 1. Count the output. Every bug and contribution is worth some points — a critical bug counts for more than a low one. Add them up to get your total points.
  2. 2. Compare it to the hours. Expected points = 20 per hour × hours worked. Your score is your points divided by that expectation. Half the expected output means a score of 50%.
  3. 3. Apply it to the pay. Base pay is untouched — you always get ₹300 for every hour. The performance amount (₹200 per hour) is multiplied by your score. Beating the expectation does not pay extra; the full amount is the maximum.

Example: 50 points of work

The same work, done in different amounts of time. Base ₹300/hr, performance ₹200/hr, expected 20 points/hr.

Hours Expected Score Base Performance Total
2.5 hrs 50 pts 100% ₹750 ₹500 ₹1,250
5 hrs 100 pts 50% ₹1,500 ₹500 ₹2,000
10 hrs 200 pts 25% ₹3,000 ₹500 ₹3,500

The 5 hr row in full: 50 points ÷ (20 × 5) = 50%. Base = ₹300 × 5 = ₹1,500. Performance = ₹200 × 5 × 50% = ₹500. Total ₹2,000.

Notice the performance amount stays at ₹500 in every row — the same work earns the same performance pay no matter how long it takes. Taking longer only adds base pay, so the value delivered per hour falls.
